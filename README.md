### 📂 Projects

#### [NeuroBridge – Neuro-Inclusive Health Assistance Platform](https://github.com/Civora-Forge/NeuroBridge)
🟢 *active — this week: refining the adaptive engine*

Neuro-inclusive health-assistance web application providing accessible, personalized support modules for individuals with diverse cognitive and learning needs. Supports role-based access for users, guardians, and support personnel, with adaptive onboarding and disorder-specific toolkits across OCD, ADHD, Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, Dyspraxia, ASD, Anxiety, Depression, and APD.

`React` `TypeScript` `FastAPI` `Python` `PostgreSQL` `REST APIs`

#### [Intrusion Detection System](https://github.com/tp-shivha-shakthiy/Intrusion-Detection-System)
🟢 *active · IIIT Kottayam, under Dr. Balasubramanian P*

Bi-LSTM achieved **99.04% binary accuracy** and **0.9792 weighted F1** on UNSW-NB15 (2.54M records, 10 attack classes). Benchmarked 7 configs across XGBoost, LR, DNN, 1D-CNN, and LSTM variants; engineered a MiniBatchKMeans + SMOTE fallback to handle extreme-minority attack classes.

`PyTorch` `XGBoost` `scikit-learn` `imbalanced-learn`

#### [VI-ReID – Visible–Infrared Person Re-Identification](https://github.com/tp-shivha-shakthiy/sthf-vi-reid)
🟡 *active · NIT Trichy, under Dr. Brindha M*

Video-based cross-modality person re-identification pipeline; evaluating retrieval performance via mAP and Rank-1 accuracy against standard benchmarks.

`PyTorch` `ResNet-50` `Deep Learning`

#### [Vanta AI – AI-Powered Women Safety Platform](https://github.com/tp-shivha-shakthiy/VantaAI)
🏆 *Runner-Up @ Girlathon 2025*

Full-stack safety platform with a conversational assistant, legal complaint generator, takedown tools, and NGO directory. Integrated Phi-3 via Ollama with SSE for offline, privacy-preserving real-time inference — built so it works even without a live connection, since that's often when it's needed most.

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `Supabase` `Ollama` `Phi-3`
